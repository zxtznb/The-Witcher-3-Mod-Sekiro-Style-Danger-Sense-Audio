## 2024-05-20 - Opt-in Visual Fallbacks for Audio-Haptic Immersive Designs
**Learning:** Purely audio and haptic-based "Zero Screen UI" designs completely exclude hearing-impaired players who are playing on Keyboard & Mouse (since they also lack controller haptics).
**Action:** When working with immersive audio-haptic systems, always design and include an opt-in on-screen visual fallback (e.g. displaying a minimal HUD message like "危") to ensure accessibility for players who cannot hear or feel the primary cues.
